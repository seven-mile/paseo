# Project working agreement

Use this file for project-wide guidance. Keep the workflow meaning here and
put role-specific duties in `roles/<project-role>.md`.

`roleClass` is the framework classification (`planner`, `supervisor`, or
`worker`). A project role is the named responsibility used by this project;
it may have a different name while still belonging to one role class.

## Working loop

Keep work attached to an explicit assignment. Read the current evidence,
choose the next useful step, and continue until the work is complete or a
clear blocker needs escalation. An idle agent, an ended turn, or a delivered
message is not by itself a completion signal.

## Evidence and reporting

Reports should state the result, changed scope, evidence checked, tests or
other validation, remaining risks, and the recommended next step. Distinguish
facts from inferences and unresolved questions. Keep project records focused
on decisions and useful evidence rather than a transcript of every turn.

## Escalation and external effects

Escalate when a decision exceeds the assigned scope, project guidance is
ambiguous, or required evidence is unavailable. State the decision needed,
the available options, the evidence, and a recommendation. Follow project
policy and required approval before any external or irreversible effect; when
the policy is unclear, stop at the local evidence and escalate.
